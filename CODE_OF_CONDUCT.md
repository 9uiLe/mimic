# Code of conduct

Mimic welcomes people who participate respectfully in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. This code applies to contributors and maintainers in those spaces.

## Expected conduct

- Be considerate and constructive. Discuss the work and its evidence, not a person's identity or motives.
- Make room for different experience levels and perspectives. Ask for clarification and accept correction.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish another person's private information or confidential material.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. This includes conduct in project spaces and related public spaces that materially affects participation in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If conduct makes participation unsafe, tell the maintainer through a private channel the maintainer has provided. Do not publish sensitive details or another person's identity in a public issue. If no private channel is available, a public issue may ask the maintainer to arrange private contact without including the incident details. A report can identify the relevant location, people involved, what happened, and any supporting links, as far as the reporter is comfortable sharing.

The maintainer will review reports, protect information as far as practical, and choose a proportionate response. Possible actions include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces. The maintainer is responsible for enforcing this code; no response time or confidential channel is promised here.
