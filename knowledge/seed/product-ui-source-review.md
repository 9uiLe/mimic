# Product UI source review

Reviewed 2026-10-09. Public first-party sources were read and the indicated
screen or slide images were viewed. The corpus links and paraphrases; it copies
no third-party image, slide, long passage, or asset.

| Case                         | Author and source                                                                                                                                                                           | What was actually checked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Observed fact and limit                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Object-first navigation      | 朴木優斗 / teamLab, [Speaker Deck](https://speakerdeck.com/teamlab/object-oriented-ui-design)                                                                                               | All 65 slides in extracted transcript; individual images for slides 9, 12, 25, 31, 48 were acquired; slide 48 was visually inspected in full. The public Speaker Deck page was also opened in the user's Mac Chrome.                                                                 | The book collection/detail example and contextual actions are the author's illustrative implementation. The slide does not prove that every work tool should start with objects. |
| Decision-focused comparison  | SmartHR Design System, [common table](https://smarthr.design/products/design-patterns/smarthr-table/)                                                                                       | Guidance on construction, object information, width, status, and actions; the linked table overview screenshot was visually inspected in the user's Mac Chrome.                                                                                                                      | It advises fields that distinguish/compare objects and explicit task status. This is SmartHR's design pattern, not a measured Mimic result.                                      |
| Function-led visual emphasis | Frank Bentley, Julia Feldman, Lennard Schmidt, Martin Pielot, Michael Gilbert / Google Design, [research article](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research) | “Expressive designs are easier to use” and “Context still matters”; the paired email screens were visually inspected in the user's Mac Chrome. The playlist counterexample was described and its image pair present in the article, but the pair was not visually inspected in full. | Google reports study-specific faster discovery of Send, while an unstructured playlist and removed labels reduced usability. Effect sizes cannot be transferred to Mimic.        |
| Hierarchical progress        | Amarilis Dias / GitHub, [Actions navigation article](https://github.blog/news-insights/product-news/improving-navigation-for-github-actions/)                                               | “Improvements to reusable workflows hierarchy” and “Local navigation”; the linked Run jobs screenshot was visually inspected in the user's Mac Chrome.                                                                                                                               | The screenshot shows parent and child jobs with statuses. It is a 2022 product snapshot, not proof of current GitHub UI or Mimic suitability.                                    |

The `obs:*` entries in `sources.json` describe the source or image. The
`hyp:*` entries are our possible transfers with conditions and risks.
Screenshots viewed for verification remain external; they are not distributed
with Mimic. A case's role for any concrete task must be assessed anew.
